where the paint lives.

Inspired by the Paintbrush Pipeline for Disney and Pixar’s Hoppers.

Keep the light.

Sample the forest’s surface colors and baked directional lighting. Each brushstroke inherits the color of its patch of scenery.

02

Anchor the paint.

Place textured cards at persistent 3D points. Switch to Per-frame to see why resampling the points makes the image shimmer.

03

Cover the journey.

One view misses what comes next. Merge points across the camera path, then change the look without changing their placement.

What this demo approximates

This independent educational demo uses procedural meshes and area-weighted surface samples instead of deep EXR data, Houdini, USD, and Nuke. Lighting is baked into vertex colors and interpolated onto brush cards. Camera coverage uses frustum tests, not visibility reconstruction. The per-frame mode deliberately resamples surface points at 12 Hz to expose flicker; the coherent mode keeps point identity, texture variation, and orientation stable. Cards face the camera and share the scene’s depth buffer, but transparent-card ordering is approximate. Reflections, clean-plate reconstruction, animated surfaces, and Pixar’s production tools are not implemented. No Disney or Pixar assets are included.
